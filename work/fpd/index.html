F%D Creative Studio

A food-design studio in Shanghai · two people

Eat Closer

F%D Creative Studio stands between food and design. We are a studio of two. We invite designers and makers to cook with a group of participants at one long table: a meal that is mostly vegetables, wastes little and uses what it has. We think that cooking and eating together is one way people come closer again.

The mark reads F%D as food: the two rings of the per-cent sign are the two o’s, F and D stand for food and design, and the stroke between the rings joins them.

Workshop 01 · 24 May 2025

Spring–Summer Food Workshop春夏食物工坊

Gua bao, the folded steamed bun, taken apart into steps anyone can do with their hands, and a hand-made cola to drink with it. The fillings were mostly vegetables: each participant brought one vegetable ingredient, and the fillings were put together from what arrived. Designed with BayBowl, a design studio; fourteen people came, two more than the poster allowed.

Workshop 02 · 20 July 2025

Pasta as Typeface把意面作为字体

The other way round: the ingredient itself as the material for letters. Fourteen people made pasta dough by hand, rolled and cut it, shaped it into the letters A to Z and photographed each one; the letters were then traced into a typeface you can type with. Designed with the type designer 五一.

Place and partners

Both workshops were held at NICE COMMUNE 好公社, 1024 Siping Road, Yangpu, Shanghai, in its NICE2035 shared kitchen, near Tongji. The kitchen is part of NICE 2035, a project of Tongji University’s College of Design and Innovation; F%D is a separate studio that used the room.

The May workshop was designed with BayBowl 湾池设计工作室, a design studio; the July one with the type designer 五一. Participants, guests and the venue agreed to appear on this page.

What this record can't say
