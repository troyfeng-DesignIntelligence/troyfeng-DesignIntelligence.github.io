01Threshold

Where do recording and sharing break down for amateur players?

Alibaba’s sports division asked for visual storytelling for amateur sport. We had a pipeline that reads a player’s body from video, and a plan to turn any clip into a film for social media in one tap. I asked four players what actually happens to their footage.

P01–P04 semi-structured interviews, 3 June → open codes → 3 themes thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006)

Events → effects

How a swing sets the timing, from the pipeline’s effect director.

  • swing peak → comic speed lines
    fast attack, slow decay: a comic burst
  • high speed + displacement → pixel ghost trail
    sustained, following the speed
  • swing start → outline particles
    always on low, bursting at the start of a swing

Checked · two tests, three changes

  • A · cognitive walkthrough

    12 peers, classmates and teammates, in four groups; each group walked through as one persona. Twice: 24 sessions.

  • B · usability test

    12 amateur players used the hi-fi prototype and thought aloud.

“Find every point where the interaction feels off, not prove the design is right.” From our test plan. We logged breakdown points, mis-taps, how long tasks took, and frustration.

  • Buttons of equal weight, too many of them: people hesitated
    → one main action per screen, on a single orange button

  • In every earlier prototype, the style you picked barely showed in the film
    → rebuilt so the choice reaches the render; otherwise the tool takes the story again, as in theme 2

  • Coach advice was generic: “nice shot, smooth out the follow-through”
    → each answer tagged “based on your match” or “general advice”

We didn’t record which test found which. Neither test was controlled, and no tester used their own footage or had to decide whether to post.

After the project · Sept 2026

A redesign, made after the project and never tested, adds what comes back after posting (Break 03) and gives TennisAtom its mark, an atom. The three exits are its states.

  • archive
  • friends
  • public

What this record can't say

  • The ball tracker's speeds were not usable, and nothing in the pipeline measures spin.
  • The numbers in the crew chat are scripted.
  • The composer, publishing and highlight ranking are unfinished.
  • Neither evaluation track was a controlled study, and no tester used their own footage or had to decide whether to post.
  • Whether a film changes what anyone posts is untested.
  • The four personas are composites synthesised from the interviews, not an empirical typology.
  • The September 2026 redesign was made after the project. Nobody tested it and it was not exhibited.

AI use

Parts of the code, the vision pipeline among them, were written with AI coding assistants, and AI assisted the data analysis. The five films were rendered with generative video tools, and the drawings on this page were made with an image model.
