Feng Kaiqi: selected works

  1. The Agent Became Me. A study of AI that shops for us. Whatever it happens to pick, the shop files as my taste; Clean Record tries to keep my profile looking like me.
  2. Zingaline. An installation: one visitor at a time, alone at a desk for six to nineteen minutes. You write down one thing you wish you could do over, and the page is shredded. A version of you made from those words takes a son’s place on the night before he leaves home. That night had been rehearsed 2,000 times before you came.
  3. TennisAtom. An AI film crew turns an amateur player's clip into a film they'd dare to post.
  4. Feeding the Cloud. A critical wearable. Each food-related upload, a photo, a search, a booking, an order, fills a second stomach a little, so the feeding of the cloud can be felt, seen and argued with.
  5. Wings & Wires. A scrolling data essay. Migrating birds are tracked with precision; people who die crossing borders are often left as a blank. I set the two records side by side to see who decides what gets written down, and what is left empty.
  6. 30-Minute Contract. Research through design on food-delivery work. The paper is under anonymous review; the full record follows the decision.

Other works

  • Visual Branding and the Politics of GentrificationAn essay on city branding, a preprint. In Barcelona I often felt the city was dressing itself for the camera; I wanted to know whether the more photogenic a city becomes, the harder it is for the people who live there to stay.2026
  • Piano WharfGulangyu is a quiet island with no cars. Everyone arrives by ferry and waits in the same hall. For that hall we made a proposal: the tide would play the island’s place-names like keys, and the loudest name would be the place to walk to next.2026
  • F%D Creative StudioA food-design studio of two. We ran workshops at one long table in a shared kitchen in Shanghai; we wanted to use food to bring people close to one another again.2025
  • Dear MonicaA letter to Mum from a mouse who lives in Shanghai. Drawn by hand, frame by frame: a portrait of how lost I felt when I first came to a city as big as Shanghai.2024
