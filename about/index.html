Feng Kaiqi

I design and research where people meet the systems that watch them: a shopping agent and the record it keeps, a family evening rehearsed by software, a film crew for amateur players, a second stomach fed by food apps. I am in the final year of a dual degree in visual communication design and artificial intelligence at Tongji University’s College of Design and Innovation in Shanghai, after an exchange semester in interaction design at Elisava, Barcelona. My first paper, a poster at HAI ’26, follows a small change on a shelf through an agent’s choices into the next recommendation.
